Our Story

Light, Love &
Transformation

Shamse — شمسة — meaning "my sun" in Arabic, is inspired by Rumi's muse, Shams of Tabriz; a story of light, love, and transformation.

Born under the Jordanian sun, every Shamse piece is proudly 100% made in Jordan, carrying a touch of heritage as a tribute to our roots, resilience, and femininity.

Because Shamse is about making it personal, playful, and yours — our handmade straps are designed to change. Pick them, switch them, mix & match, or add extras to make your Shamse uniquely you.

"Wherever you stand, be the soul of that place." — Shams of Tabriz

Our Story

Rooted in
Palestine

Shamse — شمسة — means sun. It is the name of a traditional Palestinian geometric motif, an 8-pointed star that radiates outward, found on tiles, textiles, and embroidery across the Levant for centuries.

Every piece is made with Palestinian tatreez — cross-stitch embroidery passed down through generations of women. Every bead strap is hand-strung with millefiori glass, turquoise stone, and gemstones from across the Mediterranean. No two are exactly alike.
